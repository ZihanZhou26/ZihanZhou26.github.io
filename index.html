About

My research sits at the intersection of high energy physics and astrophysics, with an emphasis on the dynamics of the relativistic compact objects in strong gravitational field.

I apply effective field theory and scattering amplitude methods in the study of black holes, relativistic binaries, tidal disruption events, and fluid mechanics, which gives new physical insight and reveals hidden underlying mathematical structure.

Recently, I have also been working on machine learning approaches for gravitational wave data analysis and on nonequilibrium quantum dynamical systems. Outside research, I am an amateur pianist and enjoy playing classical music.
