About

My work spans effective field theory, gravitational wave physics, quantum dynamics, and AI for physics.

My earlier research centered on compact object dynamics in strong gravitational field, where I used effective field theory, scattering amplitudes, and renormalization group methods to study relativistic binaries, tidal effects, and related mathematical structures in Einstein gravity.

More recently, my interests have expanded toward dynamical observables, symmetries, and nonequilibrium behavior in quantum many-body systems, as well as agent frameworks for astrophysical inference and discovery in fundamental physics.

Outside research, I am an amateur pianist and enjoy playing classical music.
