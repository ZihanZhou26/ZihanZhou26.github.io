Research

Research Interests

My research initially centered on gravitational wave astrophysics in the strong field regime, with particular emphasis on effective field theory, scattering amplitudes, and precision waveform modeling for tidal effects, relativistic binaries, and resummation in gravitational waves. More recently, my interests have broadened to include quantum dynamics and AI for physics, with particular emphasis on the interplay between dynamical observables, symmetry, and nonequilibrium behavior in quantum many-body systems, as well as agent frameworks for astrophysical inference and discovery in fundamental physics.

Core Themes

Gravity, Compact Objects, and Gravitational Waves

I study compact objects and gravitational waves using tools from effective field theory, scattering amplitudes, and precision waveform modeling. This includes relativistic binary dynamics, tidal effects, resummation methods in strong gravity, and data analysis strategies for current and future detectors, including machine learning approaches to signal modeling and search.

Research Highlights

  • We developed a systematic algorithm that bootstraps hidden symmetry representation theory directly from spectral data resolved by manifest symmetries (arXiv:2604.01296).
  • We are developing LLM agentic systems for cross-disciplinary scientific reasoning across gravitational wave astronomy, cosmology, and effective field theory (to appear).
  • With the help of AI, we discovered for the first time the “Water-hedron” in the scattering of surface water waves (to appear).
